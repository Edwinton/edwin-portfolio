From Data Fragments to Digital Portraits

The Exemption: A Digital Privacy Awareness Programme

The Exemption was a digital privacy awareness programme presented by the BVM Holy Cross College Computer Science Department and ASSET. My session introduced the hidden journey of personal data: everyday details such as location, device information, browsing activity, and interests can be gathered from different sources and assembled into a profile.

The programme explored how real-time advertising auctions and data brokers can turn those fragments into predictions and targeted ads. To make the topic hands-on, I built a team-based browser game. Teams examined a target profile, selected the relevant data packets from a larger set, and tried to reconstruct the profile.

The activity turned an abstract privacy issue into a practical challenge: each data point may seem ordinary on its own, but combining them can reveal much more about a person. The programme also included discussion of data aggregation, location tracking, behavioural analysis, and the advertising ecosystem.

I presented the session as part of the programme on 23 October 2026. The poster describes the goal as giving participants practical exposure to real-time bidding and data-broker mechanics; the programme chart lists my session as “Class by Edwin Tony.”
